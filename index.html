Task 3 Cost-Sensitive Classification

Predictive Customer Churn Classifier

Industrial customer retention analytics addressing 73.5%:26.5% subscription imbalance. Benchmarks Random Forest against XGBoost with balanced class weighting, evaluates ROC/PR trajectories, and implements threshold tuning to prioritize recall over raw accuracy.

Imbalance Benchmark (Held-Out 20% Test) Target: AUC > 0.80
Ensemble Model ROC-AUC Recall F1 Score
Random Forest (Balanced) 0.8425 0.7674 0.6247
XGBoost (scale_pos_weight) 0.8401 0.7647 0.6258
  • Exceeded target threshold with 0.8425 ROC-AUC on held-out test data.
  • Cost-sensitive weighting elevated minority-class churn recall from ~50% to over 76.7%.
  • Top churn drivers identified: month-to-month contracts, short tenure, and lack of technical support.
